5 Interactive Design Archetypes

🔍 Design Philosophy & Cognitive Ergonomics

🎯 The "Now Playing" Mental Model

Beginners understand Spotify and Apple Music instantly. By treating an active workout as a "Now Playing" track (Exercise = Song, Set = Track, Rest = Pause), zero training on how to use the app is required.

⚡ Kinetic Momentum vs Boredom

Lifting is physical and visceral. High-contrast typography and instant completion gratification provide the psychological dopamine hit that keeps lifters coming back session after session.

🌿 Zero-Border Elevated Surfaces

Traditional dashboards trap every stat inside 1px bordered boxes, creating visual claustrophobia. Elevated borderless cards use soft ambient depth, letting the lifter's eyes breathe.

🛡️ Progressive Disclosure of Complexity

Form cues, plate math, Bayesian reasoning, and warmups are kept strictly 1 tap away behind "⋯ More". Beginners never get intimidated, while advanced lifters lose zero power.

# Persona Review R2 — Priya, 27 · Fat loss · ex-MyFitnessPal

Branch `feat/overnight-build`, code-traced (no live run). Week simulated: onboarding → daily meal logging (NL quick-log, barcode, manual) → weigh-ins → coach calorie suggestion → weekly review.

## First impression
Clean onboarding, goal card says only "Calorie deficit & lean mass preservation" (`src/components/onboarding/FitnessGoalsScreen.tsx`, GOAL_OPTIONS). She finishes onboarding, accepts/declines a *workout* plan on the plan-offer screen (`src/components/Onboarding.tsx` ~L636-716 — workouts only, no numbers), and lands on Home. **Her calorie target was set silently** — `Onboarding.tsx` ~L351-361 calls `calculateRecommendedMacroGoals(...)` and `setMacroGoals(...)` with no screen ever showing her the number, the deficit size, or the expected rate of loss. An ex-MFP user notices this immediately: MFP shows the target before you agree to it.

## What worked
- **The deficit math itself is sound for her body.** `src/lib/macros.ts` `calculateRecommendedMacroGoals` (lose_fat branch): `Math.max(1400, maintenance − 450)`, protein 2.2 g/kg. Mifflin-St Jeor maintenance in `calculateMaintenanceCalories` with an activity multiplier asked at onboarding (sedentary 1.2 … very_active 1.725) instead of a hardcoded 1.4. For Priya (62 kg, 165 cm, light activity): maintenance ≈1,863 → target ≈1,413 kcal, ~450 kcal deficit. Sane.
- **The explanation exists — just late.** On Meals, `src/components/MealsPage.tsx` ~L2007-2021 renders `1,413 = 1,863 maintenance − 450 deficit · ≈0.45 kg/week` plus "Targets stay above a 1,400 kcal safety floor. Weigh yourself weekly…". This is the round-1 fix and it is genuinely good — it just isn't at onboarding.
- **Calorie-suggestion engine gating — RE-VERIFIED ✅.** `src/lib/coach-engine.ts`: `MIN_SUGGESTION_POINTS = 5`, `MIN_SUGGESTION_SPAN_DAYS = 10` (~L1085); `suggestCalorieAdjustment` returns hold when `dataPoints < 5 || spanDays < 10`. Suppression — RE-VERIFIED ✅: `isCalorieSuggestionSuppressed` (~L1316) hides the card until ≥3 fresh weigh-ins (`CALORIE_REOPEN_NEW_LOGS`) or 14 days (`CALORIE_REASSESS_DAYS`); wired in `src/lib/use-coach-digest.ts` nutrition memo, and both Apply and Dismiss in `src/components/HomePage.tsx` (~L565-595) write a `CalorieAdjustmentRecord`. Steps are small (−125/+100 kcal), never a crash cut.
- **Indian food DB is the best thing in the app.** `FOOD_DB` in `src/lib/macros.ts`: ~600 keys — roti/chapati/phulka, dal tadka/makhani/moong/toor, rajma, chole, khichdi, poha, upma, idli, dosa variants, thepla, dhokla, chaats, regional South/North dishes, Indian sweets, packaged brands (Parle-G, Maggi, Haldiram's), plus `PIECE_WEIGHTS` so "2 roti" = 2×40 g. Her typical week (poha, dal-roti, rajma-chawal, paneer bhurji, cutting chai, curd rice) is fully covered. This beats MFP's crowd-sourced mess for Indian home food.
- **Quick-log on the Meals page — round-1 fix RE-VERIFIED ✅ (this path only).** `MealsPage.tsx` `handleNaturalLogSubmit` (~L894-1020): `isJunkMealParse` rejects gibberish, `warnUnmatched` warns "logged at 0 kcal, totals may be understated", `getAbsurdQuantityWarning` refuses "100 rotis".
- **Barcode flow is strong:** native BarcodeDetector → ZXing fallback, watchdog for black-camera, manual entry always visible, not-found → editable card + AI label scan + the barcode is remembered (`src/components/BarcodeScannerModal.tsx`, `src/lib/openfoodfacts.ts`).

## Friction & bugs
- **PRIYA-1 (major, trust-breaker class): the Quick Actions logger bypasses every round-1 guard.** `src/components/QuickActionSheetModal.tsx` `handleQuickFoodSubmit` (~L101-150) has **no** `isJunkMealParse` check, **no** unmatched warning, **no** absurd-quantity check — it `addMeal()`s whatever returns and toasts success. Gibberish logs a phantom 0-kcal meal; "2 rotis and 1 slice pizza" silently logs the pizza at 0 kcal (the exact round-1 bug, alive in the second entry point); "100 rotis" logs ~11,880 kcal. Priya *will* find this — Quick Actions is the fastest path and the one a daily logger actually uses.
- **PRIYA-2 (major): coach suggestion uses the wrong goal and the wrong gender.** `src/lib/use-coach-digest.ts` nutrition memo calls `suggestCalorieAdjustment(trend, goals[0] || 'general_fitness', macroGoals)` — raw `goals[0]` (tap order) instead of `resolvePrimaryGoal` (`src/lib/types.ts` L26-33, priority-ordered, used everywhere else), and **no gender argument**, so the signature default `'male'` applies. If Priya tapped "Build Muscle" before "Lose Fat" at onboarding, the coach can suggest **+100 kcal while she is on a fat-loss target set by every other screen**. For a woman at the 1,400 onboarding floor, a −125 suggestion is also blocked by the male 1,500 floor with the confusing message "you're already at 1,400 kcal — below the safe floor of 1,500" (`coach-engine.ts` `apply()`, ~L1171-1190), contradicting the Meals page's "1,400 kcal safety floor" copy.
- **PRIYA-3 (major): Apply breaks the macro math.** `HomePage.tsx` Apply handler does `setMacroGoals({ ...current, calories: newCalories })` — protein/carbs/fat, `maintenanceCalories` and `calorieDelta` are left stale. The app elsewhere guarantees zero Atwater drift (4P+4C+9F = calories); after one Apply the Meals explanation line prints arithmetic that no longer adds up (new calories vs old delta), and the macro gram targets no longer match the calorie target she just accepted.
- **PRIYA-4 (minor): the unmatched warning is dead code on the online path.** `src/app/api/ai/parse-meal/route.ts` `validatedFoods` map (~L95-105) never sets `isUnmatched`, so `warnUnmatched` in MealsPage can only fire for the offline-fallback source. An AI-parsed item at 0 kcal passes silently unless *all* items are junk.
- **PRIYA-5 (minor): manual builder can still save a silent 0-kcal food.** The builder shows an inline "Not found in offline database" banner (`MealsPage.tsx` ~L2993), but `handleSaveMeal` (~L713) checks only absurd quantities — if she saves without typing custom macros, the food logs at 0 kcal with a success toast.
- **No weekly nutrition view (gap).** Meals is today-ring + a per-day history list; there is no 7-day calories/protein average, no adherence-by-day grid. MFP migrants live on that view. The coach's weekly review covers training, not nutrition.

## Keep-or-delete verdict
**KEEP — conditionally.** The core loop (Indian DB, NL logging on Meals, trend-based coach, honest deficit explanation on Meals) is better for an Indian fat-loss user than MFP in the places that matter daily. But PRIYA-1 means the fastest logging path silently corrupts her totals — for a user whose entire trust model is "the numbers are honest", that is one bad week away from churn. Fix PRIYA-1/2/3 and this is an easy keep.

## Competitor gap
vs MyFitnessPal: wins on Indian home-food DB and no ads/paywall nagging; loses on weekly nutrition analytics, food verification/badges, streak-by-nutrient views, and database breadth for packaged/restaurant food outside the built-in barcode catalog (Open Food Facts coverage is thinner in India).

## Rating: 6/10
